Who We Are - Thohoyandou

Mavu Clothing is a local streetwear brand based at Thavhani Mall, Thohoyandou, Limpopo 0950. Started in 2023 to provide affordable fashion R100-R500.

Our Mission

To dress Thohoyandou youth with stylish hoodies, tees and caps at affordable prices. We deliver R50 in Thohoyandou, R99 outside.
